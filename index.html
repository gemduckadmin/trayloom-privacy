Trayloom privacy policy

GEMDUCK LIMITED · Effective 25 November 2026

Your screenshots and boards

Trayloom reads the image you select or share through Android. In versions with the optional screenshot-folder shortcut, you can also grant read access to one local folder. When you open or return to the app it checks that folder for the newest image. The folder permission and last-used file reference stay on this device. Disconnect the folder in Settings to revoke access. Image decoding, square recognition and placement search run on your device. The image stays in memory while you work. The latest editable grid and shapes are kept in local app storage. Screenshots, file names, image metadata and puzzle contents are never uploaded by Trayloom. Replace the draft or clear app storage to remove it.

Optional usage statistics

Usage statistics are on by default in the initial US and New Zealand release. When device region signals indicate the EEA, United Kingdom or Switzerland, or the region is unknown, statistics start off until you enable them. Region hints are checked locally. Settings lets you turn them off at any time. GEMDUCK receives random installation, session and attempt identifiers, app version, platform, language, event time, coarse country when provided by the network, and interaction categories such as import, recognition, correction, solve outcome, optional solution feedback and recommendation interaction. We use these to understand and improve the app. There are no accounts, advertising identifiers, Firebase or advertising SDKs.

Turning statistics off stops new collection and transmission, clears unsent events and remembers your choice after restart. Turning them back on records only new actions. Offline events can wait on the device for up to seven days. Turning off statistics also hides recommendations.

Recommendations and service providers

After the final placement, Trayloom can fetch an optional recommendation from GEMDUCK's service and display its hosted media. Installed promoted apps are filtered locally. If you tap a card, Google Play receives the target app and campaign/source label. Network providers handle IP addresses to deliver and protect requests. Our analytics database does not store raw IP addresses. We use encrypted HTTPS connections and Railway to host our analytics service. We do not sell your data.

Retention and requests

Event records are retained for 90 days. Pseudonymous summaries are retained for up to 365 days since activity. Switching statistics off does not remove events already received. For access, deletion or privacy questions email hello@gemduck.com and identify the app and your local statistics ID from Settings. Clearing app storage removes local preferences and identifiers.

Trayloom is an independent puzzle tool from GEMDUCK LIMITED.
